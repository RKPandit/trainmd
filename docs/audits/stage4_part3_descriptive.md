> Read-only (author's request, 2026-10-03), after the pre-registered verdicts (all 9 CONFIRMING; FINDINGS F25) and the
> blind audit (F27). Nothing here changes a verdict, an estimand or a rule. These are the Part 3 counterparts of Part 2's
> `stage4_part2_react_vs_static.md`, `stage4_part2_followups.md` and `stage4_part2_control_false_alarms.md`.
> **Source:** the local releases `results_release/stage4_part3_{static,react}/` only (one trial per cell, pilots
> excluded), tables generated by `scripts/part3_descriptive.py`.
> **Definitions:**
> - "detected" is the scorer's `detected_predicted`.
> - Static detection is the mean over a case's repeats, then over cases; ReAct has one trial per case.
> - "Opened" is read from the ReAct tool traces: `read_code` on `train.py`, and `read_config` / `diff_config` /
>   `read_code` on a config.
> - Opening is the agent's own choice, so the conditional rates are associations, not effects of opening.
> - J = non-crash faulty detection − control false-alarm rate (healthy and benign pooled).
> - "Value changed" / "key added" are the benign forms (an existing key's value changed, or a new key added).
> - The image workload has no `datautil.py`: every fault's gated path is in `train.py` (L40).

## Reading (descriptive)

1. **Code opening.**
   - **No ReAct trial detected a silent fault without opening `train.py`:** 0/31 under `off`, 0/4 under `stats`.
     This is Part 2's pattern again (Sonnet 0/19 there).
   - **Sonnet (thinking off), metric inflation, `off`:** ReAct opened `train.py` in 17/36 cases. It detected 0.71 when
     it did and 0.00 when it did not, against 0.96 for static, which always has the file. Part 2's figures were 0.78
     vs 0.00 against 0.97–1.00.
   - **Opening is not sufficient.**
     - Luna `medium` opened `train.py` in 32/36 leakage cases and detected 0.38 (static 0.88).
     - Luna `none` opened it in 98–99% of silent-fault trials and detected 0.33 (`off`) / 0.38 (`stats`), against static
       0.62 / 0.97.
   - **Under `stats`:** opening is near-universal, and ReAct detection is at or near static's except for Luna `none`.
     This is H14's gain (F25), seen in the raw rates.
   - **Opening tracks suspicion for Sonnet.** On controls, Sonnet opens `train.py` in 15/104 (`off`) and 8/104
     (`stats`) trials, against 89/108 faulty trials (`off`). Haiku and both Luna conditions open it on almost every
     control.
   - **Under `stats`, Luna `none`'s ReAct false alarms all come from trials that opened `train.py`** (26/103 vs 0/1).
     Its `off`-arm rate is 3/100. Opening plus a bare reference, without reasoning, gives false alarms (F26(b)).
2. **Compliance is high in every condition.**
   - Haiku ReAct hit the turn limit without submitting in 6/460 trials. Luna `medium` ReAct ended without submitting
     in 6/460. Luna `medium` static had 6 unparsed submits and 1 no-submit after the follow-up. Luna `none` ReAct had
     1 of each.
   - **Malformed evidence refs:** Luna `none` static 79/1,068 (7%); Sonnet static 21/1,068.
   - **Correct diagnosis without a repair:** Sonnet (thinking off) static 78/709 (11%; Part 2: 6%). Every other
     condition × agent is ≤ 4%.
   - Missing fields are rare (19 `evidence_refs`, 8 `diagnosis`), and no submission had an ignored field.
   - Unsubmitted and unparsed trials score as misses, so the detection rates are conservative by those counts.
3. **Control false alarms.**
   - **By form: no consistent effect.**
     - Healthy, value-changed and key-added rates are within a few points of each other in every condition except
       Luna `none` static `off`, where healthy controls are flagged most (12/20 vs 13/48 and 11/36).
     - The healthy controls' configs are the verbatim, commented copies (L43); with n = 20, this is noted, not
       interpreted.
     - Pooled by type, the lr 0.05 → 0.04 change (23/144 static, 12/96 ReAct) and the schedule no-op (19/144,
