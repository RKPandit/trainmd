# Citation audit and author sign-off

**Audit drafted:** 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** every scholarly work currently named or linked under `docs/`  
**Submission rule:** only rows with a date in **Author verified on** may be cited in a manuscript.

This is a claim-level provenance ledger, not merely a bibliography. Tooling may collect metadata,
open primary pages, and flag mismatches. The project author must personally open the linked primary
source and check the title, complete author list, venue, year, identifier, and the specific claim
for which TrainMD uses the source. Only then may the author replace `PENDING` with the date of that
check. A date in **Tooling primary-source check** is not human certification.

For mutable preprints, repeat the author check immediately before submission and update the date.
If the preprint changes materially, record the version checked. If a source is listed as unresolved
below, either complete and sign off its row or remove the associated claim from the manuscript.
